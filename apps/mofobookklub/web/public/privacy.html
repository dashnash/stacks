Privacy Policy

Last updated November 7, 2026

MOFO Book Klub ("the Klub", "we", "us") is a private, invitation-only website for a group of friends to plan book club meetings, suggest and vote on books, and talk about them. This policy explains what information the site collects at mofobookklub.dashnash.ninja, why, and what choices you have. It is a small, non-commercial club: we don't sell anything, show ads, or sell your data.

Information we collect

From your Google account, when you sign in. We use Google Sign-In. With your permission, Google shares these basic profile details with us:

  • your name,
  • your email address, and
  • your profile picture.

We do not receive your Google password, and we do not ask for access to your Gmail, contacts, calendar, Drive, or any other Google data.

Things you add to the site. This includes the books you nominate, your votes, the comments and replies you post, and, if you lead a meeting, the details of that meeting (date, time and video-call link).

Basic technical information. Like most websites, our hosting provider (Amazon Web Services) keeps short-lived server logs. These can include your IP address, browser type and the pages you requested, and are used only to keep the site running and secure.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and confirm you are an approved member. New sign-ups wait for an admin to approve them.
  • To show your name and picture next to your nominations, votes and comments to other members of the Klub.
  • To run the club: scheduling meetings, keeping The Lineup of nominated books, and keeping a history of past reads.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell, rent or trade it to anyone.

Who can see it

The site is only available to signed-in, approved members. Your name, picture and what you post are visible to other members. Your email address stays with the shared Stacks sign-in, which uses it to identify your account: the Klub's own pages never receive it. Admins who manage the Klub's members can see it on the Stacks access page. Nothing you post is public on the open web.

Services we rely on

  • Google, for sign-in. Google's use of your data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Amazon Web Services, which hosts the site and stores its data in the United States.
  • Open Library and Google Books, which provide book titles, authors, descriptions and cover images. We send them only book searches and ISBNs, never your personal information.
  • Amazon.com. Book pages link to Amazon. If you follow a link, Amazon's own privacy policy applies on their site.

Cookies and storage

We don't use advertising or analytics cookies. While you are signed in, your browser keeps your sign-in token in session storage, which is cleared when you close the tab or sign out. Google Sign-In may set its own cookies to remember your Google session.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and what you post for as long as you are a member, so the Klub's history stays intact. If you ask us to delete your account, we will remove your profile and can remove or anonymize your comments.

Your choices

  • You can edit or delete your own comments at any time.
  • You can withdraw books you nominated and take back your votes.
  • You can ask us for a copy of your information, or ask us to correct or delete it, using the contact details below.
  • You can disconnect the site from your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections.

Security

The site is served only over HTTPS. Data is stored in Amazon Web Services with access limited to the site itself and the Klub's administrators. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Children

The Klub is for adults. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. For significant changes, we will also let members know on the site.

Contact

Questions or requests about your data: email dashiell.nash@gmail.com.
